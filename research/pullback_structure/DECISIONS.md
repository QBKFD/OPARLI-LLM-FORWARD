# pullback_structure: implementation decisions

Choices made while coding PREREG.md (merged in 42675bb) where the PREREG
doesn't spell out the mechanics. None changes an approved value. Each is
tested in `test_pullback.py` where marked.

| id | decision | why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | After the turn, Q1's first passage (SL vs pullback_high, P12) and the search for a close above LH (Q2) run independently over the 30-candle horizon. A close above LH counts for Q2 only if no 1-min low < SL came before its close. The gallery label is the first in time: B or C if such a break came, else A if Q1 is A. | §3.8: "B and C: on every close above LH before a new lower low". When pullback_high > LH, a break can come before Q1 is decided; stopping at Q1 would lose it. |
| D2 | When the turn led to no lower low and no break within the horizon, the event ends at the close of candle turn + 30 ("no_break" or "unresolved"). | P17's horizon, applied to the whole event. |
| D3 | A close above LH counts from the candle after the swing low, including the k confirmation candles; a lower low and the turn count from the candle after the confirmation (tested: `test_break_during_confirmation_counts`). | §3.4 says "before the turn"; no lower low can come during confirmation (P5). |
| D4 | P18: an event ends at its decisive 1-min bar or at the close of its last tracked candle. A new event in that direction needs a confirmation close strictly after that. | One live event per direction. |
| D5 | P24: an event not finished inside the data is dropped from every question (kind `truncated`), even when one of its questions was already decided. | "an event … is dropped". |
| D6 | B vs C: a 1-min bar reaching the B target and the same or an earlier bar going below SL makes C; a lower low inside the W window makes C and the event leaves Q3 (`ll_during_w`, counted). | The tie rule of P13, applied to B. |
| D7 | Bootstrap p-values: one-sided p = (#{resamples ≤ 0} + 1) / (B + 1); Q2's two-sided p = 2 × min of the two tail shares, each +1-corrected, capped at 1. Each test seeds its own generator from BOOT_SEED (20261027) plus a fixed offset (Q1: +0, Q2: +10, Q3: +20; before Amendment 1, Q1 bucket b used +b). | P27, P28; reproducible per test. |
| D8 | A candle's scheduled minutes are its real length in New York time, so a candle across a DST change has 180 or 300 (tested: `test_candles_ny_anchor_dst_and_drops`). | P3, "scheduled open minutes". |
| D9 | The swing highs used for the structure and for LH are those that occurred before the swing-low candle. | P6, P7: confirmed by the swing low's confirmation, so they occurred at or before it. |
| D10 | σ (P9) is `stage1.state_table`'s `sigma_px_240` at the label equal to the candle's end (the state as of that close). A start without a finite σ is refused and counted (`no_sigma`). | squeeze_oco's test checks that table against `vol_block.state_at` + `sigma_over`. |
| D11 | Step 2 checks P30 on **turns** (per bucket originally, pooled after Amendment 1), not on resolved events. Resolution is an outcome, and step 2 reports none. | §6.2 counts before any outcome; turns ≥ resolved, so a bucket short on turns is short on resolved events too. |
| D12 | Random walk (§5): path r uses `default_rng((20261027, r))`; each path's Q1 CI uses bootstrap seed 20261027 + 1000 + r. The simulated σ_px at a candle's close is the simulated close × the real `sigma_ret_240` at that label. Bars open at the previous close; high and low are the larger and smaller of open and close. The path starts at the first real close of the loaded data (tested: `test_sim_candles_match_candles`, `test_simulated_bars_are_consistent`). | §5 gives the real timestamps and σ. σ_px must scale with the simulated price, as it does with the real one. |
| D13 | Amendment 1: the BH part of the pass rule (P32) is kept, so PASS also needs BH p < 0.05 over Q1, Q2, Q3. The holdout runs only if dev is PASS, as P31 did for the fixed buckets (tested: `test_verdicts`). | The owner: "pass rule unchanged". |
| D14 | The original step 2 output stays in `results/counts.json` (the stop). The amended step 2 writes `results/counts_a1.json`, with the dev depth tercile cut-points used for the descriptive terciles. | The record of the stop is kept. |
| D15 | `run_variants.py` (the owner's count-only comparison) applies W, X, the horizon and P18 in candles of each variant's timeframe. 1h candles are UTC hours with ≥ 50 % of their minutes; σ is the pinned 240-min HAR at the candle's close. These variants are not PREREG settings. | Counts only, so the variants differ in timeframe, k and swings alone. |
| D16 | Amendment 2: `params.TIMEFRAME = "1h"` selects `pullback.candles_hourly` in `common.prepare`. The 4h builder stays for the record and for `run_variants.py`. Step 2 writes `results/counts_a2.json`, and the random walk and dev read that file (tested: `test_hourly_candles_and_drops`, `test_params_are_amendment_2`, `test_two_swings`). | §12.1. |
| D17 | §11.5's ATR(14) is computed on the study's candles, now 1h (Wilder, as of the confirmation close). | §12.2 keeps the robustness line and leaves its timeframe with the study's. |
